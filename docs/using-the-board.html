Using the board

Old Skool Board

Board layout

The board shows your Jira board's own columns, with the same names, in the same order. A column can hold more than one status, just as it does in Jira. Columns with "Backlog" in their name are left out.

One column is expanded at a time. Click any column header to expand it. In the expanded column, each parent item is pinned on the left with its sub-tasks arranged into lanes that follow your Jira sub-task workflow. The other columns show their cards in a compact list. Long column and lane names are shortened; hover over one to see it in full.

Parent items appear as yellow cards. Sub-tasks appear as blue cards.

If a work item's status isn't in any column on your board, it appears under Unmapped work items below the board. To bring it onto the board, add that status to a column in your Jira board settings.

Moving parent items

Drag a parent item to another column to change its status. This triggers the same Jira workflow transition as moving it on your native Jira board. When a column holds more than one status, it splits into one box per status while you drag, so you choose exactly which status the work item gets.

Pause on a status box and the column switches back to its cards with a blue insertion line, so you can pick the position as well as the status. A column with a single status shows its cards and insertion line straight away, and the column you're dragging from starts as cards so you can reorder within it. Or drop straight onto a box in another column to add the work item at the end of that column. Parent item order is saved to Jira's own rank, so Old Skool Board and your Jira board show the same order when they load.

When a move changes status, you have a moment to press Cancel (or Escape) before it's sent to Jira. The board handles one move at a time; if a move is still finishing, wait a moment and try again. If a transition isn't allowed by your workflow, you'll see a message and the card stays where it was.

Reordering parent items needs a board filter ordered by Rank and Jira's Schedule work items and Edit work items permissions. Without them, multi-status columns stay as boxes, no insertion line appears, and if you try to reorder the board tells you why. A missing permission is only discovered the first time you try; card order is read-only after that. You can still change status: the work item is placed at the end of the column for now, and follows your board's own order after a reload. See Known limitations.

Moving sub-tasks

Drag a sub-task between lanes in the expanded column. Each lane corresponds to a status in your Jira sub-task workflow. You can also reorder sub-tasks within a lane by dragging them up or down. A blue line shows where the sub-task will land.

Sub-task order within a lane is kept by Old Skool Board, not Jira, and is shared by everyone in the project.

Creating items inline

Hover near the bottom of a column and click + Create to create a parent item, or hover a sub-task lane beside a parent item and click + Create there to create a sub-task. Type a title and press Enter to save, or Escape to cancel.

If the column holds more than one status, pick one from Choose a status before creating. Columns showing No statuses mapped have no create option. On Scrum boards, new work items go to your Jira backlog, not the active sprint, so they won't stay on the board until they're added to the sprint.

Opening work item details

Click any card title to open the work item's details without leaving the board. You'll see the description, sub-tasks, comments, and key details: status, assignee, reporter, priority, labels, and dates.

To open the work item in Jira instead, click its key (e.g. CSS-10) at the top left, or Cmd/Ctrl-click the card on the board.

Hiding completed items

On company-managed Kanban boards, you can hide completed items that haven't been updated for a set period. Click the settings cog in the top-right corner of the board and choose a Done retention period: 1 week, 2 weeks, 4 weeks, or Keep all.

This setting is saved per board, for your account. It isn't available on Scrum boards or team-managed boards.
